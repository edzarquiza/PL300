Question 1

Visualize and analyze the data
Medium

A finance manager wants to understand how the company’s operating profit
changes throughout the fiscal year.

The report must display: - the opening balance, - individual monthly
increases and decreases, - and the ending balance.

Which visualization should you use?

A. Clustered column chart
B. Line chart
C. Scatter chart
D. Waterfall chart

Answer: D

Explanation

A waterfall chart is designed to show how sequential positive and
negative values contribute to a final total.

-   A is incorrect because a column chart compares values but does not
    illustrate cumulative increases and decreases.
-   B is incorrect because a line chart emphasizes trends over time.
-   C is incorrect because a scatter chart analyzes relationships
    between two numeric measures.
